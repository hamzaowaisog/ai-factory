# Clinic front desk portal

A small product for clinic front-desk staff: a web app, and an API with a local database behind it.

- Staff sign in with their email address.
- After signing in, staff see today's appointments: patient name, time and status.

Two screens only: sign in, and today's appointments. The appointments are stored in the database and read through
the API. No real authentication in this version: any valid email signs in.
